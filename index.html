Mammut

Your studio’s command hub. Data in ice, agents in amber.

Mammut is a Mac app for indie app developers. It brings your RevenueCat revenue and your PostHog product analytics into one calm cockpit, so you see how your apps are doing without switching tools.

How your data is handled

It runs on your Mac

Mammut connects through a small backend on your own Mac. Your sign-ins, keys and synced numbers are stored there, in files only your user account can read.

It only reads

Mammut asks for read access only. It can’t create, change or delete anything in your PostHog or RevenueCat account.

No Mammut servers

Your data, sign-ins and keys never reach tioocoo studios. This page and Mammut’s sign-in description are static files that collect nothing.

You stay in control

Disconnect a source in Mammut at any time, or revoke Mammut’s access in your PostHog or RevenueCat account.

What Mammut can read

When you sign in, the provider shows you this access before you approve it.

PostHog

Projects (read)
Lists your projects so you can pick one. project:read
Dashboards (read)
Reads the one dashboard you choose to show in Mammut. dashboard:read
Insights (read)
Reads the results of that dashboard’s insights, such as daily active users or sessions. insight:read

Mammut reads only the dashboard you choose and has no write access.

RevenueCat

Overview metrics (read)
Reads your project’s overview numbers, such as monthly recurring revenue, revenue and active subscriptions. charts_metrics:overview:read
Projects (read)
Lists your project names so you can pick one. project_configuration:projects:read

Mammut doesn’t ask for customer or subscriber details and has no write access.

Privacy

What is stored, and where. The backend on your Mac keeps the latest synced numbers for each source, plus one backup a day for 30 days. Sign-ins and keys are kept in a private configuration file on the same Mac. Nothing is sent to tioocoo studios or anyone else.

Where your data goes. Your Mac talks directly to PostHog and RevenueCat to fetch your numbers. Mammut has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

The optional setup assistant. If you turn it on, your chat messages and Mammut’s connection status are sent to Anthropic, using your own Anthropic API key, to answer setup questions. Keys and tokens are never included: text that looks like one is blocked on your Mac before anything is sent.

This page. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, scripts or trackers. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as for any site it hosts.

Deleting everything. Disconnect your sources in Mammut, then delete the backend’s folders on your Mac: ~/.config/mammut, ~/.local/share/mammut and ~/Library/Application Support/MammutBackend.

Disconnecting Mammut

  1. In Mammut, open Connections and click Disconnect on the source. Its sign-in or key is removed from your Mac at once.
  2. To also end the sign-in on the provider’s side, revoke Mammut’s access in your PostHog or RevenueCat account settings.
